## Constitutive law and potential

With the explicit positive dynamic viscosity mu from the borrowed owned state's material, let g=G u be the parent directed derivatives. Each normal row with fluid volume V has tau=2 mu g. Each unordered cross pair at the exact same edge, quadrant and ascending axis pair has equal stored sector weight w, engineering shear gamma=g_ab+g_ba, and both stresses tau_ab=tau_ba=mu gamma. A flat-wall pair includes its stationary normal-component zero row; its stress is generally nonzero even though its transpose has no active columns. Preserve every represented row.

Define the partial Rayleigh dissipation potential R=mu sum_normal V g^2 + mu/2 sum_shear w gamma^2. Define D=2 R. Both have power units W, not stored mechanical energy. The matched face-integrated generalized force is F=-G^T W tau. Its exact finite-real work is u dot F=-D=-2R. The directional first variation of R is v dot G^T W tau, and the quadratic matrix is symmetric positive semidefinite for positive weights and viscosity. A skew affine rotation on a compatible interior patch has zero symmetric stress; individual cross derivatives can be nonzero. This local compatibility claim does not say a global rotation satisfies stationary obstacle traces.

The flat-wall ray uses the parent's represented center-to-wall separation delta and actual fluid sector weight. The contribution is F=-mu w U/delta^2 and D=mu w (U/delta)^2. Summed conductance is sum(w)/delta^2; identifying this with a geometric area divided by delta requires an additional exact product volume hypothesis. No snapping, missing sector, corner stencil or wall traction interpretation is introduced here.

The constitutive and variational motivation comes from Batty and Bridson, *Accurate Viscous Free Surfaces for Buckling, Coiling, and Rotating Liquids* (2008), sections 3–5, https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rbridson/docs/batty-sca08-viscosity.pdf, and Batty, *A Simple Finite Difference Method for Time-Dependent, Variable Coefficient Stokes Flow on Irregular Domains* (2010 preprint), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1010.2832.
