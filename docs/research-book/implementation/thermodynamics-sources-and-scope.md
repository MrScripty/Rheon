## Reading progression and practical conclusion

Read Chapters 41–47 in order after the continuum/MAC/transport foundations and Chapters 26–28. A fixed boiling-temperature switch is inadequate: phase equilibrium depends on pressure/composition/curvature, while transfer rate depends on heat/species supply and possibly interface kinetics. Unequal liquid/vapor densities produce volume and a nonzero divergence constraint. An incompressible sealed box cannot accommodate arbitrary net evaporation without pressure/compliance or another modeled exchange.

The strongest staged path is conservative single-fluid heat transport and boundary accounting, then bounded Boussinesq convection, then a narrow resolved unequal-density phase-change problem. One interface mass-transfer rate must consistently drive phase inventory, interface speed, energy and pressure volume source. Variable-density low-Mach gas, thermal particles, wall nucleation and strong acoustic/flash/collapse regimes require separate closures and validation. No universal boiling model or solver winner is asserted.

The validation ladder is proposed. No thermal solver campaign, hardware result, performance measurement or new machine-checked proof is supplied. Some benchmark bodies/tables remain unread; their exact targets must be verified before implementation acceptance. Shell/public-source access failures and lawful accessible alternatives are recorded below and in the rights audit; none is represented as a successful protected access.

### Portal and reservoir dependency, without portal edits

Read-only [PR148 Chapter40](https://github.com/MrScripty/Rheon/blob/4b0cec9265a968385dc1361de75023e696d8a927/docs/research-book/chapters/40-fluid-portals-and-open-boundaries.md) distinguishes periodic topology from open reservoirs and already warns that appearance tracer is not energy. A thermal extension needs matching enthalpy/species advection at periodic seams, conductive seam coupling, incoming reservoir state, outgoing interior state and absolute background pressure. Total-energy reservoir flux includes advected enthalpy plus kinetic energy and the selected diffusive/viscous terms; it must not add pressure work twice.

Prescribed incompressible boundary volume flux must match thermal/interfacial dilation. A sealed system needs gas pressure evolution or another explicit compliance mechanism. Particle birth/exit requires mass, momentum, energy, composition and identity accounting. These are dependencies for later jointly reviewed work; no portal plan, chapter, index, code or publication receipt is modified here.
