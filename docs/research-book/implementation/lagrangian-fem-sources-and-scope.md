Book author: **Puma**. Publication audit: 21 October 2026. This guide accompanies chapters30–36. ## Checked mathematics and remaining qualification

Independent reasoning review checked the material/weak-form identities, chosen solid and gas stress/tangents, exact tetrahedral mass and force arithmetic, implicit work identity, pressure-wall reactions, gradient/injectivity and same-box arguments, thermal semidiscrete power, ALE weak fluxes, capillary derivatives and fixed-interface work. Conditional proofs require the named exact-real, integration, regularity, constraint and domain assumptions. The computed geometry diagnostic is not an outward-rounded floating-point certificate. Hand calculations are not executed numerical evidence.

The publication revision preserves those equations and proofs while replacing two code-shaped recipe blocks with practical prose. No new theorem is machine-checked, no FEM solver is compiled or run, and no native/hardware verification is claimed. Normal book/Pages checks validate the actual publication artifacts at their stated scope; they do not establish FEM accuracy by rendering these chapters.

Remaining advanced work includes actual moving mixed-family stability/consistency; thermal/shock time integration and entropy; selected discrete ALE flux/GCL/remap; moving/distributed contact and FSI solution; and independently bounded spatial/time refinement after implementation. Existing fitted-height and restricted fitted-ALE studies remain narrower work and do not fill those gaps. No admission manifest or numerical acceptance threshold is broadened by this publication.
