## Scope chosen before implementation

Use one bounded event loop for the existing frictionless sphere driver and the
isolated Coulomb response. Keep the existing frictionless public request,
records, callbacks and returned-result behavior. Add a typed Coulomb interval
request/record/report using that same query, progress, budget and departure
machinery. Each accepted coast/impact segment publishes atomically; later refusal
retains the accepted owner/record prefix and positive remaining time. A terminal
miss can complete after the impact budget is consumed, as before. Completion
means all requested time was consumed, not merely that a budget was exhausted.

The collider is the declared COM sphere, not the moving render/traction mesh.
Its radius and exactly isotropic stored mass/moment remain fixed. Orientation
and spin do not change this shape. During each unforced segment V and omega
are constant, c(t)=c0+t V, and the actual pose/mesh is regenerated by the existing
PR35 coast. Friction changes the next segment's V and omega, so requery from the
actual published owner and reapply its rotation/clock/geometry admission. There
is no genuine new physical blocking assumption within this declared subset.
An orientation-dependent mesh collider, moving support or general inertia would
need different event/dynamics assumptions and is outside this integration.

[Mirtich's original thesis](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jfc/mirtich/thesis/mirtichThesis.pdf)
separates collision detection, response and static-contact/microcollision models.
Its general frictional response can change sliding direction and uses a fuller
integration law. [Baraff's rigid notes](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~baraff/sigcourse/notesd2.pdf)
derive rigid contact-point impulse kinematics. Here the previously derived
[isotropic fixed-contact Coulomb disk](isolated-sphere-friction.md) remains the
declared constitutive law; repeating its isolated events does not implement
either reference's general collision or resting-contact system.

## Actual departure and unfinished cases

At the last accepted hit, renew the existing private certificate on the entire
actual pose/body snapshot, static stamp, radius, facet and point p. Its exact-sign
kernel checks represented-data clearance |c-p|^2>=R^2, facet support
(c-p) dot (x-p)<=0 at all three vertices, and COM outward speed
(c-p) dot V>=0. No spin sign is required: the sphere shape is rotationally
invariant, while tangential material-point velocity does not move its COM shape.
The actual next endpoint must also satisfy the outward separating-plane sign
before publication. Existing exact-real support proofs apply independently of
the chosen impact law. No formal IEEE/kernel refinement is added.

This skips only the certified last facet. Older touching facets still refuse.
Initial touch/overlap, simultaneous/ambiguous events, inward or unsupported
rounded post-impact data, radius mismatch, nonrepresentable arithmetic,
nonprogressing clock/remaining time, cancellation and later excessive rotation
remain stops. In particular, e=0 does not create a resting force or persistent
no-slip constraint. A friction kick can make omega large enough that the next
coast refuses; accepted previous impacts remain visible. Rounding can make the
exact departure predicate fail despite the real radial theorem; report that
failure and unused time rather than snapping geometry or velocity.

## Finite energy, momentum and work ledgers

Each event uses the actual point q and lever r=q-c. Its normal-plus-tangential
impulse J changes both V and omega; spin torque is total r cross J. No normal-only
intermediate owner is published. In exact matched geometry, fixed mass m>0,
isotropic moment I>0, unit n, radial r=-d n, approaching normal speed vn, e in
[0,1] and mu>=0 give the already proved split kinetic increment

    DeltaT = -(m/2)(1-e^2)vn^2 - qt s + (k/2)qt^2 <= 0,
    k=1/m+d^2/I, s=|vt|, qt=min(s/k,mu Jn).

Free coasts preserve exact kinetic energy and spin, with world-origin angular
momentum preserved because (t V) cross (m V)=0. For a finite accepted sequence,
linear momentum change equals sum J, spin change equals sum(r cross J), and
world-origin angular change equals sum(q cross J). The energy increments and
midpoint point work telescope. Conditional nonincrease follows event by event;
it does not prove convergence, collision completeness, persistent contact or
IEEE safety. Exact d=R physical correspondence remains an additional premise.

Report separate nearest-rounded sums of impulse, actual-point spin/world moment,
predicted full-energy change, actual event change and midpoint point work. Compare
them with actual interval endpoints. Clock and nominal-duration defects remain
separate. Aggregate accounting can be unavailable on overflow while raw accepted
records remain intact; these diagnostics cannot authorize further advancement.
