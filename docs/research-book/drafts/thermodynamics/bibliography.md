# Primary bibliography, precise support and access limits

Research access date: **21 October 2026**. These are primary papers, issuing-body releases, official project source or the authors' institutional records. Search results and third-party aggregators were used to locate primary targets, not as equation authorities. A title/abstract-only entry is not treated as a full-body technical review. Original derivations in Chapters41–47 stand on their explicitly stated assumptions; no third-party proof text is reproduced.

The [rights audit](source-license-audit.json) is the companion authority for intended use. Citation is not permission to reuse prose, figures, tables, software or datasets. Article text, numerical-result tables, illustrations, pseudocode and implementation code are not incorporated. No quoted paper passage is needed by the manuscript.

## Thermodynamics and properties

[Official release PDF](https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/IAPWS-95.download), [issuing-body record](https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/IAPWS-95). Read selected §§2–4, Table3 and saturation/validity discussion; cover permission inspected. ## Interface-resolved transport and numerical models

Zwick (1954).** *The Growth of Vapor Bubbles in Superheated Liquids*, Journal of Applied Physics25(4),493–500, [primary DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1721668), [Caltech author record](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/dhgp6-86k43). Metadata/abstract inspected, full body unread. Classical bubble-growth provenance only. No unverified similarity coefficient or test tolerance imported. N2/N3 supply the inspected benchmark-family descriptions instead.

The Fourier-mode, series-resistance, frozen thermal-graph, equal-density Stefan and restricted spherical diffusion-drop formulas in Chapter47 are explicitly derived in the manuscript. These mathematical references do not require copying a textbook's expression, diagram or code. A future implemented liquid–vapor benchmark must match its actual density, boundary, pressure and heat/species assumptions.
