The particle pressure-gradient energy diagnostic is a mechanical potential check under its assumed pressure law. It is not an evolving thermodynamic internal-energy equation. Likewise a carrier kinetic-energy/work report is useful mechanical evidence, not total thermal energy. The gas preset's ideal EOS uses temperature but does not prove that compression raises that temperature.

## What the existing book already owns

Chapters2–10 establish continuum balance, MAC projection, transport, interfaces and forces. Chapter11 distinguishes SPH previews from PIC/FLIP/APIC specifications and records current particle limitations. Chapters19–25 address boundaries, volume, viscosity, wetting and capillarity. Chapters26–28 supply compressible state/EOS/waves and a scoped numerical/validation research extension. Chapters30–36 are the separate published material/FEM progression. They are not overwritten or silently retitled by these drafts.

New Chapters41–47 connect those foundations to thermal storage/transport, heat-driven motion and pressure/composition-dependent liquid–gas transfer. GPU37–39 and portal40 retain their separate ownership/status. No thermal result is inferred from a GPU design or FEM exposition.

## Portal and reservoir dependency, without portal edits

Read-only [PR148 Chapter40](https://github.com/MrScripty/Rheon/blob/4b0cec9265a968385dc1361de75023e696d8a927/docs/research-book/chapters/40-fluid-portals-and-open-boundaries.md) distinguishes periodic topology from open reservoirs and already warns that appearance tracer is not energy. A thermal extension needs matching enthalpy/species advection at periodic seams, conductive seam coupling, incoming reservoir state, outgoing interior state and absolute background pressure. Total-energy reservoir flux includes advected enthalpy plus kinetic energy and the selected diffusive/viscous terms; it must not add pressure work twice.

Prescribed incompressible boundary volume flux must match thermal/interfacial dilation. A sealed system needs gas pressure evolution or another explicit compliance mechanism. Particle birth/exit requires mass, momentum, energy, composition and identity accounting. These are dependencies for later jointly reviewed work; no portal plan, chapter, index, code or publication receipt is modified here.
