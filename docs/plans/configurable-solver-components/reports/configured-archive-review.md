## Source basis

`bound_configured_run` validates the actual constructor snapshot against the frozen intent before forming a closed typed provenance declaration and delegating to the existing configured recorder header. The parent helper extraction remains a pure conversion of actual `SessionConfiguration` and grid into the same frozen constructor representation. It does not construct a second Session, infer future controls, change native schema-one framing, or create a new numerical/SQLite owner.

Cold header validation independently compares exact recording limits, configured profile, bounded public constructor-header decode, counts/spacing/origin, expected run/source identity, every constructor control through bit-preserving serialized comparison, and the complete frozen intent digest plus executor declaration. It runs before publication iteration. Empty/failed-start archives therefore receive the same header checks even when there is no constructor/accepted row to consult.

Publication validation delegates to the existing full typed recording audit, retaining its actual interval-start continuity, pressure-prior hash/history, finite accepted-field and receipt checks. The binding then enforces exact expected generation/stage sequence, optional actual constructor rest/full-liquid fields, explicit stage presence and exact viscosity bits, and actual native/configured preferred-provider schedule. Configured intervals require the exact complete declared provider identity and actual attempted/context presence; unsupported/quality fallback remains legitimate accepted native output and is not silently relabeled configured inference.

Actual accepted durations may shorten below requested duration; they cannot exceed it. The code preserves recorded owner starts/ends instead of generating `ordinal*requested_dt` or subtracting duration from an endpoint. Completed requires every declared publication and the exact requested accepted-step horizon. Failed/Cancelled may have fewer durable rows and a greater explicitly reported accepted tail, within the requested horizon; they do not fabricate missing fields. An absent accepted tail prevents pinning/Ready rather than becoming zero. This permits honest capacity failures while rejecting completed-short or out-of-range evidence.

`pin_closed` requires the caller to close the writer, refuses repinning, hashes a bounded regular archive before opening and after dropping the verified reader, and updates the final pin only after success. `open_member` independently reopens and verifies exact profile/header/status/count/outcome/accepted-tail and typed contents, checking the pin before and after full Ready validation. Missing, NotRun, active Incomplete, closed Unpinned, Unsupported and Invalid remain distinct and return no Ready reader. Active/unpinned archives receive header and generic/full typed checks, but do not become verified frozen-future Ready evidence simply because their current prefix is readable.

Protected immutable local roots/files remain a prerequisite. Relative path and symlink-component checks plus double hashes detect ordinary changes; they are not authentication or protection against arbitrary concurrent filesystem manipulation after the final check. Cold inspection does not resolve the deployment, load a model, construct a Session, advance numerics, or substitute missing arrays.

The frozen digest binds declared future forces, requested duration, volume controls and external workspace limits; recorded RHS hashes and accepted outcomes do not independently reconstruct or prove those inputs. Only the header and receipt facts actually persisted by their owners can be checked. Production composition must still allocate/limit the declared workspaces and drive the same frozen inputs through the existing Session/recorder owners.
