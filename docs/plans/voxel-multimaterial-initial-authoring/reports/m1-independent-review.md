## Inspected numerical and ownership contracts

**Variable coefficients reach the pressure operator.** `with_face_densities` validates field lengths, positive finite densities and representable normal acceleration/weight coefficients before returning borrowed immutable coefficients. Its constructor cannot combine those coefficients with slab/column geometry. `neighbor_weight`, `face_weight`, the full operator path, diagonal, correction and both existing f32 correction entry points all select the face-density branch. The `surface == None` condition continues to identify a full pressure graph/gauge; it no longer alone selects uniform weights. Positive shared interior weights on the connected full-box graph establish the gauge-free SPD structure by the edge-energy identity. The tests additionally compare a heterogeneous matrix product with an independently assembled dense graph and recover a manufactured gauged pressure at contrasts 1, 2, 10, 100 and 1000.

**Legacy paths retain their owner.** Ordinary scalar constructors initialize no face-density view, retain their cached uniform weights/diagonals, and enter the original arithmetic branches. The new full-box f64 helpers reject a slab/column operator rather than borrowing its incompatible geometry implicitly. Existing f32 correction APIs remain available and now also use the correct coefficient if explicitly paired with the new variant. No legacy state representation or existing pressure solve semantics were rewritten.

**SGS classification is closed without a source edit.** Its existing `fixed_box_weights` consumer receives `None` for the variable-coefficient operator and falls through to `neighbor_weight` and `diagonal`, including its lower/upper faces. M1's owning `MaterialProjectionWorkspace` constructs the existing default Jacobi workspace; it does not advertise SGS qualification. The inspected SGS code has no remaining production unwrap of the uniform-weight option on this route; the unwrap seen in the file is in a scalar-operator test fixture. This is source reasoning, not executed heterogeneous SGS parity evidence.

The new cfg-parallel unit fixture `variable_full_box_declines_uniform_sgs_weights` directly checks the owning classification, the variable neighbor coefficient and the unchanged ordinary scalar classification. Its purpose is appropriately narrow; it does not claim a full SGS solve result. The new module README accurately distinguishes the state/projection component, unavailable coupled/native behavior, borrowed success scratch and aggregate payload exclusions.

**Pressure compatibility and gauge remain explicit.** The existing `solve_rhs` validates all full RHS values, checks closed-domain sum compatibility before gauge elimination, fixes pressure cell zero, and evaluates the true residual on the complete graph, including the gauge cell. The new caller neither subtracts a mean nor adjusts the material state to repair incompatibility. `build_rhs_f64` uses signed integrated face flux over dt; correction uses the same face rho and physical spacing. `divergence_f64` separately differentiates stored f64 faces in physical units and the owning projection rejects an excessive actual result. Pressure is mechanical relative pressure, not gas EOS pressure.

**State and coefficient semantics match M1.** Construction accepts only 4–64 cells per axis, a matching finite F in [0,1], positive normal mixture density and representable cell mass. F=0 retains the captured positive gas density. Material coefficients are copied values; Water/Air construction does not re-read presets on projection and imports no particle EOS, temperature or surface-cohesion evolution. Interior face density is the arithmetic average of the adjacent cell densities, evaluated as two half contributions. Boundary densities use the adjacent cell but do not create dynamic wall faces; wall velocities must be exactly zero. Partial fractions are documented as a numerical component/fixture interface; the later native binary authoring conversion remains a separate required gate.

**Projection success is not a committed timestep.** The state is borrowed immutably, all writes go to workspace scratch, and the returned `ProjectedVelocity` borrows only success-qualified scratch. Failed validation, cancellation, pressure failure or actual-divergence failure cannot mutate accepted F/velocity/pressure/time/generation. The candidate is explicitly documented as not an accepted step. Cancellation is checked before coefficient work, by the existing pressure loop and after correction/divergence, before any success value is returned. M2 must still own the atomic coupled generation and pressure provenance; the current component does not pretend to provide that transaction.

**Managed payload accounting includes PCG.** State preflight charges two cell arrays plus one full MAC field and checks actual Vec capacities after fallible allocations. Projection preflight adds its two cell scratch arrays, two MAC fields and all six PCG cell vectors to the supplied state's actual allocation. It computes the remaining cap before constructing PCG, then checks the actual aggregate again. No independent full-cap allowance is granted to every field. The current report means managed payload only, excluding allocator/process/GUI overhead. M2 must extend the aggregate to its newly introduced transport/stress/candidate buffers; M1 accounting is not evidence that the complete future method fits a selected application cap.

## Test evidence inspected and its limits

The seven new contract tests are bounded component fixtures (six in the initial round plus the targeted T1 addition below). The dense graph assembly uses explicit Cartesian edges, its own cell/face arithmetic and V/(rho h²), rather than the production diagonal/weight evaluator. Symmetry and nonconstant positive edge energy are checked, manufactured pressure is compared after gauge subtraction, and an intentionally incompatible RHS must return `IncompatibleRhs`. The hydrostatic fixture independently integrates adjacent half-column masses under gravity on every axis, with actual Water/Air as well as selected coefficient contrasts. Its velocity and pressure-difference assertions are stronger than checking a still image. The arbitrary-material rest fixture confirms zero forcing returns rest and gas carries mass.

Negative fixtures inspected target an invalid fraction cell, invalid material density, state/workspace cap failure, nonzero wall face, nonconvergent zero-iteration solve, initial cancellation, coefficient zero/NaN and wrong shape. State-preservation assertions inspect F, velocities, pressure availability and generation. The owning state is immutable by construction during projection; these tests therefore corroborate the actual boundary rather than an assumed rollback path. The broader M2 rejection/cancellation/accepted-state tests remain necessary once there is a mutable coupled step.

**T1 — Low, initially nonblocking; resolved by targeted rereview.** The initial constant-density correction fixture exercised only the in-place f32 route and therefore could miss a removed heterogeneous dispatch in either public f32 entry. The added `heterogeneous_f32_corrections_match_each_independent_face_gradient` uses distinct positive per-face densities and a nonuniform cell-pressure pattern, calls both `correct_velocity` and `correct_candidate_in_place`, and computes every expected interior correction directly from independently indexed adjacent cells and the declared rho/spacing. Both components are checked against that value, including exactly zero fixed-wall values (the zero expected value yields a zero tolerance). The two-f32-epsilon relative allowance covers the two algebraically different evaluation orders before final f32 rounding; it does not permit a scalar-density dispatch to pass. This closes the specific population gap without using one production correction as the other's oracle. The implementer reports seven new plus fifteen legacy focused contracts and the two operator unit fixtures passed, with strict parallel all-target Clippy; this reviewer inspected the test and did not independently rerun those commands. No f32 native-mode, performance or SGS qualification claim follows. Owner: implementer; final disposition: **resolved, no further required correction in this targeted scope**.

The tests do not establish finite-volume transport, cumulative phase conservation, mass/momentum consistency, interface refinement, variable-viscosity stress, complete hydrostatic stepping with nonzero unequal viscosity, immutable native rerun or any graphical interaction. In particular, the projection-only hydrostatic fixture does not execute the viscosity/gravity ordering; that deciding regression belongs to M2 as already required by the plan.
