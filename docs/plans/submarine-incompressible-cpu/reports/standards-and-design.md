## Adopt or own the bounded numerical slice

Requirement: a native opt-in safe-Rust CPU lab for fixed aligned-body creeping flow with affine full-vector farfield/no-slip, actual pressure/shear load quadrature, caller-bounded retained payload, cancellation/rollback and independently inspectable binary64 fields. Generic sealed/reduced solvers do not satisfy the boundary contract. This decision is limited to I1; difficult arbitrary-hull/convection/moving-boundary expansion requires renewed comparison and independent method review.

No dependency is added by this plan or proposed I1. Validator starts with Python stdlib rational/dense/analytic routines written originally for declared fixture mathematics. New NumPy/SciPy/meshing/VTK tool use later needs exact version/license/consumer qualification; optional dependencies cannot be inferred from ambient installation. The plan's dependencies are an admission policy, not a completed transitive-license audit for future code.

## Uncertainty and stopping condition

Observed source facts justify a fixed aligned synthetic-body lab, not submarine predictions. An accurate pressure trace and conservative force quadrature remain deciding design risks. Manufactured stress/load and the recorded affine-pressure counterexample must fail a naive adjacent-cell recipe before any force consumer is admitted. If that cannot be achieved within I1's boundary or cap, stop and replan the numerical operator; do not relax accuracy after observing outputs.

SUBOFF configuration/rights and turbulent closure do not affect an original synthetic-body Stokes lab, so they block only experiment ingestion/promotion. This makes the first step independently runnable without representing laminar low-Re evidence as turbulent validation.
