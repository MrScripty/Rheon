Recorded facts must stay distinct from predictions

In this prototype every status is authored. A rejected pressure candidate with an accepted fallback is a separate event from accepted publication. Missing timing, residual or provenance stays unavailable.

Source contract references used for this design
  • Configured Session recording: field association, units, interval pressure, owned receipts and missing-history semantics.
  • Configured runtime recording: RCP3 observations and separate RCT1 terminal custody; unavailable facts remain absent.
  • ContextPressureProvider: stateless candidate support, binding, host history and cancellation.
  • LiquidTransport: prepare carrier → volume → final BeforeCommit → publication.
  • Architecture proposal: typed future stage/subgraph/whole-solver seams and receipt-derived view.
