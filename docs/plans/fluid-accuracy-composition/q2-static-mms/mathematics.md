# CV3-A continuum and integration contract v1

This is a prospective derivation, without selected numerical execution. All
primitive constants and geometry coordinates are exact represented binary64
values before R2 arithmetic. In SI units rho=1 kg/m3, dt=1 s, mu=binary64(.1)
Pa s, L=1 m, U=1 m/s and extrusion H=1 m. The characteristic stress is
max(mu U/L,rho U L/dt)=1 Pa. Native loads derive from these primitives, not
independently rounded .2/.4 coefficients. u_star is identically zero.

With X=x/L, Y=y/L and psi=UL(X^2Y+XY^2), u=(d_y psi,-d_x psi) has units m/s
and equals U(X^2+2XY,-2XY-Y^2). Its gradient is
(U/L)[[2X+2Y,2X],[-2Y,-2X-2Y]], with identically zero divergence.
p=tau(1+X+2Y+XY) has units Pa. Thus

```
sigma_xx = -p + 4 mu U/L (X+Y)
sigma_xy = 2 mu U/L (X-Y)
sigma_yy = -p - 4 mu U/L (X+Y)
div sigma = (2 mu U/L^2 - tau/L (1+Y),
             -2 mu U/L^2 - tau/L (2+X))
f = (rho/dt) u - div sigma
g = sigma n_outward
```

f has units N/m3 and g N/m2. Their integrals times extrusion have units N;
moments have N m and contraction with velocity has W. Pressure is absolute
under total natural traction: no constant row removal, mean subtraction or
closed-wall gauge is selected. Integrate actual f and g, independently of any
desired discrete solution. The equations are A U-B^T P=F_body+F_boundary,
B U=0, A=M/dt+K, with full consistent M and original full rounded A preserved.

## Forced work and momentum

For u_star=0, multiply the first equation by U. Using the full stored operators,

```
U^T M U/dt + U^T K U - P^T B U
    - U^T F_body - U^T F_boundary = U^T r
```

where r=A U-B^T P-F. Export both load vectors, direct physical body/boundary
power, resultants and torques separately. Kinetic energy E=.5 U^T M U has
the forced step identity 2E+dt U^T K U-dt(bodypower+boundarypower)
 =dt P^T B U+dt U^T r. There is no unforced energy-decrease requirement.
Rigid virtual fields (1,0),(0,1),(-y,x) have zero symmetric strain and divergence.
Their momentum/moment contractions compare inertia with both continuum loads.

## Reference integration degree

Every common half-cell patch carries scalar Q1 polynomials of total degree2;
their derivatives degree1. f and sigma have degree2, so body phi*f and oblique
edge phi*(sigma n) have degree at most4. Split edges at ALL half-cell component
knots (which also contain primal pressure-cell planes). Each resulting edge has
one local derivative and liquid-side pressure polynomial; use its midpoint only
to identify the patch, never to average pressure or stress.

New boundary loads use positive three-point Gauss-Legendre on each segment,
degree5-exact in real arithmetic. CV1's original two-point constant-stress trace
maps remain unchanged and cannot qualify the new quadratic traction.
For body loads, the existing3x3Duffy triangle rule maps a degree4 polynomial to
degrees at most5 in r (including the r Jacobian) and4 in s. It is sufficient in
real arithmetic, while R2 exact moments measure all native rounding separately.

Velocity/P0-pressure L2 squared errors have degree<=4 on common volume patches.
Stress-traction L2 squared errors have degree<=4 on common edge pieces. Exact
rational polynomial moments followed by outward-enclosed square roots supply
independent squared integrals, without reusing native quadrature points.
L1 Euclidean-vector magnitudes involve square roots and are not polynomial.
They require independently bounded subdivision/integration and a retained
uncertainty interval; quadrature sampling alone is not a bound.

## Reference bound selected for prospective review

For a represented/exact rational KKT target C and rational approximate inverse R,
compute E=I-RC and delta=||E||_infinity EXACTLY. If delta<1, C is invertible and
for rational candidate z, r=b-Cz gives

```
||z_exact-z||_infinity <= ||R r||_infinity/(1-delta).
```

This follows by the Neumann series for (I-E)^-1. R may be generated by
highprecision LU but its accuracy is not assumed; only the exact defect check
supports the bound. Apply separately to native rounded-target R1 and exact
integral R2. No guaranteed-bound claim is made until the actual checks execute
successfully within hard resources. Propagate the resulting coefficient bounds
through Q1 basis/derivative and P0 stress reconstruction into every deciding
norm/refinement interval. A failed defect or unresolved L1/error budget refuses.

Primary references: [NIST DLMF Gauss quadrature](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.5#v)
for quadrature order, and [Rump2010, Theorem1.1 and equation10.9](https://www.tuhh.de/ti3/paper/rump/Ru10.pdf)
for verified inverse-defect/residual bounds. The precise rational implementation
and resource feasibility require independent prospective review; citing a theorem
alone does not supply an executed certificate.
