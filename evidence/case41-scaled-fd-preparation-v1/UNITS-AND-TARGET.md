## Residual and mixed unknown units

The native equation accumulates integrated generalized momentum and divides all 22 entries by h. R maps coefficient velocities to nodal velocities, with dimensionless affine weights. Consequently every reduced rate r_j has force units M L T⁻², and is conjugate to velocity coefficient z_j under discrete work. These are test-basis generalized forces; individual r_j are not cellwise acceleration, divergence or pressure error. Components2 and3 are conjugate to left- and middle-cap horizontal velocity, respectively. Their 74.1% raw squared-norm share depends on the declared basis; it is not a basis-independent physical error share. ## Two fixed diagnostic metrics

Use L*=1 (period), U*=1 (original left-cap velocity scale), T*=L*/U*=1, M*=27/8 (actual conserved fixture mass), a*=U*/T*=1, F*=M*a*=27/8, and Pi*=F*/L*=27/8. These scales are declared independently of the tiny residual; none is fitted to pass a test. Define x=Cx xhat, with six acceleration scales a* and sixteen pressure-coordinate scales Pi*. The primary dimensionless linear model is Jhat=J Cx/F*, rhat=r/F*. It transforms the unchanged raw sphere equivalently to ||rhat||₂≤1e-13/F*. The native gate itself is untouched.

The second diagnostic is the physically motivated dual-mass norm: ||r||_W²=rᵀWr, W=M⁻¹/(M* a*²). It measures generalized load through the kinetic mass form. Under an invertible velocity-basis change C, r'=Cᵀr and M'=CᵀMC imply r'ᵀM'⁻¹r'=rᵀM⁻¹r. This is a local exact derivation, checked by an exact congruence fixture. It does not supply a replacement scalar threshold: the original Euclidean sphere becomes an ellipsoid under this metric. No diagnostic score supplies acceptance.

For captured pressure columns B, the W-orthogonal projection is P_W=B(BᵀWB)⁻¹BᵀW. The reader checks positive exact LDL pivots of M, BᵀW(I−P_W)r=0 and weighted Pythagoras. Both order16 and32 captures are analyzed. Exact rational Gram inverses are diagnostic oracles; a eventual floating-point condition/singular-value reader must use QR/SVD or a verified metric factor and must not mistake Gram conditioning for the original matrix's. LAPACK distinguishes vector/matrix norms and matrix sensitivity explicitly. [LAPACK error measures](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/lug/node75.html).

Six acceleration columns are still missing. Existing B/M/D captures can determine pressure range/complement in these metrics, but not rank, singular directions or conditioning of J=[A B], nor rank of (I−P_W)A. The frozen seven-equation contract adds only one unchanged baseline and six prescribed order16 probes; it applies no correction. Its nominal delta includes the dimensional numeric acceleration scale 0.01 in the current units. It is a source rule for this fixture, not a coordinate-invariant differentiator. Record actual representable offsets separately without changing that denominator.

## What would justify this target at this state

First declare the physical accuracy sought: allowed cap-position error, velocity error in the mass norm, and reconstructed pressure error in its spatial norm. Pressure coordinates cannot be compared directly to a pressure budget. Translate coefficient sensitivities through U=Rz, q(s), and Q(q)Pi, including Q's dependence on geometry. Set these budgets from discretization/reference resolution or the intended physical use, independently of this refusal.

A candidate force-term cancellation ratio |r_i|/sum_k |term_ki| is useful scale evidence, but it is not the backward error of the nonlinear discretization. The earlier group sums also aggregate internal cancellation. For an **already computed** linear-system solution s of J s=b, componentwise linear backward error is max_i |b−Js|_i/(|J||s|+|b|)_i, with an explicit zero/safe-minimum policy. Native pivot growth, true residual, scaled reciprocal condition and forward-error estimates should accompany it. LAPACK DGERFS implements this distinction and includes safe-minimum terms; DGESVX exposes separate backward error, estimated forward error, reciprocal condition and pivot-growth diagnostics. None certifies a nonlinear root or justifies a universal target. The new fixed-candidate columns cannot be combined with an old correction from a different iterate to manufacture a BERR. No new correction is computed here. [DGERFS implementation](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/double/dgerfs.f), [DGESVX documentation](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/d5/dbe/group__gesvx_ga82173a93234afc15d70b64233b3e5bc8.html).

For this nonlinear state, first identify the intended real-arithmetic finite-discretization map F on a smooth, fixed-rank neighborhood. The rounded native program is not a smooth map, and the exact replay of captured binary fields is not automatically this real map. Bound their discrepancy explicitly. Obtain a trustworthy local derivative model of F and separate bounds for residual evaluation error, FD error and nonlinear remainder. In declared dimensionless coordinates, an inverse bound K for the true derivative plus a bound ε_F for evaluation error yields a local linear sensitivity estimate K(||rhat||+ε_F), not yet a root-distance theorem. If a known neighborhood has an invertible average derivative along the segment to an established root, norm at most K, then the integral mean-value identity gives root distance ≤K||Fhat(x)||. Alternatively, with Lipschitz derivative constant L_J, root distance e in a declared basin satisfies e≤K||Fhat(x)||+(K L_J/2)e². Existence, basin radius and derivative bounds are additional hypotheses; mere pointwise invertibility or an observed small correction supplies none. These statements are derived here by integrating the derivative and bounding Taylor remainder, not asserted Lean/IEEE results.

For nominal one-sided delta, finite-difference error contains truncation (bounded using a derivative-variation bound), representable-offset distortion, and (ε_F(x+delta)+ε_F(x))/|delta|. The prescribed six samples expose their linear response but cannot establish derivative-variation or whole-path evaluation bounds by themselves. Geometry, pressure-basis rank, donor switches, sign partitions and checked range behavior must be covered. Existing order16/32 agreement and finite-H point checks are observations, not uniform error bounds. Normal IEEE estimates require their explicit normal-range/rounding hypotheses; subnormal or checked-division refusals remain actual failures. [Research arithmetic/tolerance limits](../../docs/research-book/chapters/15-floating-point.md).

To mathematically support keeping this target as a sufficient physical criterion, bound the propagated state errors at the target by the declared budgets, with evaluation/derivative/remainder uncertainty included. To assess arithmetic feasibility, give upper bounds showing that residual-evaluation uncertainty is small enough for reliable classification around the unchanged target. Neither analysis requires a larger target. No bound of this kind is established by the current captures; no lower arithmetic floor is claimed. Physical `1e-11`, quadrature `1e-15`, GCL/work rules and original failures remain independently authoritative.
