The constraint and the work

At a compatible no-slip endpoint, u = w before and after the step. Its reaction impulse is J = −dt Funconstrained, in N s. Work delivered to the liquid is w · J, in joules; its sign is retained. Relative reaction work J · (u − w) is zero. Bulk viscosity still dissipates energy, and a finite Navier wall also dissipates relative slip energy.

The ledger includes the nonzero initial energy of the compatible moving endpoint, explicit-step energy correction, and stored-f32 rounding. A steady reference is drawn at every snapshot to make relaxation visible. Deviation from the discrete equilibrium combines unfinished relaxation, explicit transient error and rounding; it does not isolate time integration error or establish temporal order.

The original endpoint basis extends each first/last wet-center speed constantly to its physical wall. Thus its constrained trace is exact, while the continuum profile at wet centers has spatial endpoint error. For two no-slip walls the references are u(y)=y and uj=j/(L−1). With a finite lower Navier wall, they are u(y)=(y+ℓ)/(1+ℓ) and uj=(jh+ℓ)/((L−1)h+ℓ), with ℓ=μ/β.

Scope of this laboratory

These are three native runs, each with 8192 explicit steps through 96 s. The controls select actual stored profiles and do not interpolate or recompute a trajectory. The geometry is fixed, lateral directions are periodic, normal motion is zero, and tangential fields are uniform within each layer. Initial endpoint traces must already match prescribed fixed wall speeds.

No wetting, adhesion, contact-line dynamics, arbitrary collision-mesh fluid contact, moving normal walls, time-varying prescribed speeds, variable-density inertia or evolving free surface is modeled. Two no-slip walls sharing a single wet node are refused because their separate reactions are nonunique. The lab supplies a bounded refinement comparison, not temporal-order qualification or a proof of Rust floating-point behavior.
