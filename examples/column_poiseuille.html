The actual explicit step

Each wet dual node has volume V = Aω and mass m = ρV. Acceleration a (m/s²) gives body force ma; force density q (N/m³) gives Vq. They agree only when q = ρa. Interior viscosity adds μA/h times the neighboring velocity differences. A free node advances by dt times total force divided by mass. At each compatible fixed no-slip endpoint, reaction J = −dt(total unconstrained force), including body force, keeps its increment zero.

Body work is dt Σ fbody · u using the incoming speed. The ledger retains its sign, wall work, viscous loss, the separate ½Σmδ² explicit correction and stored-f32 rounding. From rest, old-speed body work is zero at the first step while increment energy is positive. Stationary walls supply zero work but transfer momentum.

Steady and finite-time references

With constant ρ, μ and q = G, fixed H = A = 1, zero normal motion, periodic lateral directions and stationary walls, continuum steady speed is u(y) = Gy(H−y)/(2μ), with flow per unit width GH³/(12μ). A body force is used here; no pressure gradient is solved. At fixed G, steady speed is independent of density. At fixed a, G = ρa and density changes both steady amplitude and the viscous relaxation rate μ/ρ.

The retained endpoint basis extends wet-center values constantly to the physical walls. With h = H/L, its distinct equilibrium is uj = Gh²j(L−1−j)/(2μ). The exact-real discrete startup curve is the closed-form sine-mode solution of this explicit recurrence from zero: each error mode multiplies by 1−4(μ/ρ)dt/h² sin²(kπ/(2(L−1))) per step. This curve is evaluated in binary64; it is not a second integrated trajectory or a continuum transient. Native deviation includes f32 storage, native assembly rounding and reference sin/power/sum rounding, without a certified reference-evaluation bound. Steady endpoint refinement is not temporal-order evidence.

Bounded scope

Every case starts from rest and runs 8192 steps to 96 s at dt = 96/8192 s. The API adds one uniform tangential vector only; normal forces, regions, arbitrary mesh fluid topology, variable-density inertia, pressure coupling, evolving interfaces, adhesion and contact lines are outside this operation. All generated results stay outside Git. Conditional Lean statements cover explicit force-unit, reaction, work, momentum and quadratic-stencil algebra, with step/assembly assumptions visible; they do not certify Rust IEEE behavior or the spectral reference.
